How a word is checked

Two independent sources, and you see whether they agree.

  1. 1

    Scholars annotated it

    The Digital Corpus of Sanskrit records each word's dictionary form and grammar by hand: adhikāraḥ → adhikāra, prathamā, puṃliṅga, ekavacana.

  2. 2

    Pāṇini's rules check it

    Vidyut, a working implementation of more than 2,000 sūtras of the Aṣṭādhyāyī, looks the form up and tests the same analysis. For verbs and participles it also derives the word, sūtra by sūtra.

  3. 3

    You see the result

    They agree. They differ, so the word is open for review. The rules have no entry yet. A compound member, not yet checked.
