How a word is checked

A scholar's analysis, checked against Pāṇini's rules.

  1. 1

    Scholars annotated it

    The Digital Corpus of Sanskrit records each word's dictionary form and grammar by hand: adhikāraḥ → adhikāra, prathamā, puṃliṅga, ekavacana.

  2. 2

    Pāṇini's rules check it

    Vidyut, a working implementation of more than 2,000 sūtras of the Aṣṭādhyāyī, tests whether its rules allow that analysis for this form. Where they do, it also derives the word sūtra by sūtra. The rules say what a form can be; choosing among the possibilities in context is still the scholar's reading.

  3. 3

    You see the result

    The rules allow the analysis. The rules analyse the form differently, so it is open for review. The rules have no entry yet. A compound member, not yet checked.
