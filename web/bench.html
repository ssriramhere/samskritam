Samskritam benchmark v0

A Sanskrit grammar test with answers fixed by Pāṇini's rules.

Models write Sanskrit that looks fluent. This measures whether the grammar underneath is right: building verb and noun forms, reading them back, parsing words in real verses, and scanning meter. Every answer comes from a rule engine or from two independent sources, never from another model.

The five tasks

What the model is asked

Real questions from the item set. The model must answer in JSON; the grader accepts IAST or Devanagari.

Tiṅanta

150 questions

Build a finite verb from a root, lakāra, person and number.

Question

Give the verb form of √vah (bhvādi, "to carry") in laṅ (imperfect), uttama-puruṣa, ekavacana, kartari.

avaham · avahe either is accepted

Analysis

100 questions

Chandas

Method

Where the questions and answers come from

  • Questions are generated from fixed templates by bench/generate.py with a fixed seed, so anyone can rebuild the identical set.
  • Verb questions use 54 common roots, each pinned to its dhātupāṭha entry so the gaṇa and meaning are unambiguous, across seven lakāras: laṭ, laṅ, loṭ, vidhiliṅ, lṛṭ, liṭ and luṅ.
  • Noun questions use the most frequent noun and adjective stems of the Bhagavad Gītā.
  • Answers for verbs and nouns are derived by Vidyut, an implementation of more than 2,000 sūtras of the Aṣṭādhyāyī. Where Pāṇini allows more than one form, every form is accepted. Each noun answer is also checked against Vidyut's dictionary of inflected forms; two questions failed that check (the dual of mahat and the feminine of bhagavat) and were removed.
  • Answers for the Gītā questions are the hand annotations of the Digital Corpus of Sanskrit, used only where Vidyut agrees with them.
  • Answers for meter are computed from the rules of prosody. The last syllable of each pāda is not graded, since either weight is allowed there.
Grading

What counts as right

  • The exact form is required. A near miss is wrong.
  • IAST and Devanagari are both accepted, and so are both spellings of a nasal before a consonant (saṃjaya and sañjaya).
  • For Gītā questions, the lemma and every grammatical field given by the annotation must match. Common citation forms are accepted for pronouns (yuṣmad for tvad, for example). The boundary between noun and adjective is not graded.
  • An answer that is not valid JSON in the requested shape is wrong.
Limits

What this does not measure yet

  • Meaning, translation, commentary and śāstric reasoning. Those need human judgement.
  • Sandhi splitting, compounds and kāraka roles. These are planned for the next version.
  • The answer key is only as complete as Vidyut. A valid form it does not generate would be marked wrong, which is why wrong answers are reviewed by hand before results are published.
  • The Gītā is famous and well represented in training data, so the Gītā and Chandas tasks may partly measure memory. The verb and noun tasks mostly ask for forms that rarely occur in any text.
Use it

Run it on your own model

The items, the runner and the grader are in the GitHub repository. The runner needs only Python; the grader also needs the vidyut package.

Download the 498 questions (JSONL) 
pip install -r pipeline/requirements.txt
python -c "import vidyut; vidyut.download_data('vidyut-data')"
export OPENROUTER_API_KEY=...
python bench/run.py --provider openrouter --model <vendor/model> --rpm 18
python bench/grade.py bench/results/<file>.jsonl
