It is derived from the Digital Corpus of Sanskrit by Oliver Hellwig, itself
licensed CC BY 4.0. When you reuse it, credit both:

> Samskritam.ai, built on the Digital Corpus of Sanskrit (Oliver Hellwig,
> https://github.com/OliverHellwig/sanskrit), CC BY 4.0.

Changes made to the source annotations: word analyses were re-checked against
the Vidyut implementation of Pāṇini's grammar and tagged with the result;
verse numbering was mapped to the common (vulgate) numbering; meter,
derivations and learner-facing labels were added.

## Exception: lines from the critical edition of the Mahābhārata

In the Mahābhārata, verse numbering and the choice of lines follow the critical
edition. A few lines that the Digital Corpus of Sanskrit lacks are shown from the
critical edition's electronic text, which is © Bhandarkar Oriental Research
Institute, Pune, 1999 (https://bombay.indology.info/mahabharata/statement.html).
These lines are marked on the site and in `web/data/mahabharata/` (verses with a
"note" naming them). They are not covered by the CC BY 4.0 license above;
permission to show them has been requested from BORI.
